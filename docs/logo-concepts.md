# Logo concepts

These early directions are kept as a record of the visual exploration. The current header uses a custom SVG mark based on ordered tracks and a checked transfer. Any future PNG export must use RGBA with alpha zero outside the glyph and stay readable on light and dark backgrounds.

1. Minimal icon: Spot2YTMusic, a careful music playlist migrator. Three audio bars connected to an arrow, flat single-color geometry, high contrast, SVG-friendly, no text. Transparent background, PNG with alpha channel (RGBA), no canvas fill.
2. App icon: Spot2YTMusic, a careful music playlist migrator. Rounded square outline holding a music note that crosses a small bridge, clean geometry with restrained depth, readable at 32 pixels, no text. Transparent background, PNG with alpha channel (RGBA), no canvas fill.
3. Wordmark: Spot2YTMusic, a careful music playlist migrator. Clear lettering with a simple bridge and sound-wave mark, opaque glyphs, high contrast on light and dark surfaces. Transparent background, PNG with alpha channel (RGBA), no canvas fill.
4. Emblem: Spot2YTMusic, a careful music playlist migrator. Compact badge with two linked music notes and a short bridge span, high contrast, simple shapes, no text. Transparent background, PNG with alpha channel (RGBA), no canvas fill.
5. Abstract: Spot2YTMusic, a careful music playlist migrator. Two ordered columns of dots joined by a single flowing line, suggesting songs keeping their order, high contrast, SVG-friendly, no text. Transparent background, PNG with alpha channel (RGBA), no canvas fill.
